About the Department

Southern University Bangladesh began as an institute in 1998 and was granted full university status in 2002 under the Private Universities Act, with its permanent campus in Arefin Nagar, Baizid Bostami, Chattogram. Operating under the motto "Committed to Academic Excellence," the Department of Computer Science & Engineering was established within the Faculty of Science & Engineering to give students a strongly lab-oriented foundation in software engineering, data networks, and information systems, alongside real-project experience in a hands-on IT team environment.

Academic Activities

  • Regular seminars and workshops on emerging technologies (AI, IoT, Cybersecurity)
  • Inter-department programming and idea-fair competitions
  • Industry-academia collaboration through internships and guest lectures
  • Active IEEE-affiliated student branch and alumni mentorship network
  • Undergraduate research groups supervised by faculty members

Admission Requirements

  1. Physics and Mathematics required at SSC/O-Level and HSC/A-Level
  2. Minimum CGPA of 2.5 in both SSC and HSC (or combined 6.0)
  3. Submission of online application with academic transcripts
  4. Departmental admission test and interview
